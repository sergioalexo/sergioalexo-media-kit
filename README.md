## Sergio Alexo

**Sergio Alexo** is a Winnipeg-based DJ originally from Ukraine, known for delivering **pure, uplifting energy** through dynamic and versatile sets.

His musical journey started in childhood, learning to play songs on piano by ear using online videos. At 14, inspired by a friend who introduced him to music production, Sergio began experimenting with FL Studio. Without any formal training, he created his first track—*“Unnamed”*—relying entirely on instinct, marking the beginning of his self-taught path.

By 16, Sergio stepped into DJing after getting his first controller, quickly developing his skills. However, his progression was interrupted by the war in Ukraine, forcing him to relocate and rebuild multiple times. After moving to Canada, he paused music for nearly two years to focus on adapting, learning the language, and building a stable life.

His return came through an opportunity from Greg, owner of *SHO Coffee Bar*, who gave him a platform to start DJing again. What began as unpaid sets turned into a key growth period, where Sergio developed his crowd-reading ability and transitioned into **house music**, shaping his signature feel-good style.

Determined to take things further, Sergio invested in professional gear and began building his brand. After connecting with Timur, a DJ and event organizer behind *Lost Dancing*, he was invited to perform at *Dreams*, marking his first club appearance. From there, he expanded into private events, weddings, and cultural parties, eventually becoming a paid resident DJ at SHO.

Today, Sergio performs multiple times per week and continues to evolve his sound, currently exploring **hip-hop and R&B** to expand his musical range before defining his long-term direction.

Whether playing an intimate venue or a packed dance floor, his mission remains the same:  
> **Create unforgettable moments where people can lose themselves in the music.**
